# Farmhands of SPLORR!!

A small turn-based farm on an 8 by 8 field (demo). Plant crops, hire farmhands, keep them fed. A fed farmhand makes every crop around them grow twice as fast. Harvest into the barn, then sell or keep crops back as seed.

Odin compiled to WebAssembly; run `tools/serve.sh` and open http://localhost:8000/.
