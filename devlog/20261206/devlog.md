# Farmhands of SPLORR!! is on itch.io

*Draft for December 6, 2026 (not for the post: this line). Post type: Major update or launch. For the picture, use itch's hosted copy of the cover or the `02-farm.png` screenshot once the page has it. Not approved by the user yet.*

Farmhands of SPLORR!! has been playable on GitHub Pages since November 25. Today it gets a page here too.

It's a small turn-based farm on an 8 by 8 field. Ye start with four squares of soil and twenty coins.

- **Crops:** ten of them, from radishes to pumpkins. Ye start with carrots and buy a license for each of the others, in any order; the more a crop earns, the dearer its license.
- **Farmhands:** a fed one doubles the growth of every crop around them. A hungry one sulks, and a starving one quits. Move them where they're needed, or let them go.
- **The barn:** sell the harvest, keep some back as seed, and feed the hands from what's left. Tell it how many seeds to keep and how much to hold before selling, and it gets on with it. Build it bigger and the rats move in. Buy a cat, and another when it wanders off.
- **Crows:** they eat whatever a scarecrow isn't watching. Each scarecrow guards a 5 by 5 patch, until the weather knocks it down.
- **Land:** every square ye buy makes the next one dearer.
- **Soil:** each crop takes a nutrient and gives back another. Rotate them, or spread manure. And don't leave a ripe crop standing too long: it rots.

A short tutorial walks ye through the first farm, the Help tab is the manual, and the Stats tab keeps count of everything.

It's a demo, with no goal but a bigger farm. It saves in yer browser, plays with keys, mouse, touch or a gamepad.

The latest version always lives at https://playdeezgames.github.io/farmhands-of-splorr/ and the itch build can lag behind it.
